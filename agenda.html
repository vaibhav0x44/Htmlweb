Computers : For Non CS Bachelors

Agenda

The main agenda of the "Computers: For Non-CS Bachelors" project is to provide a simple and beginner-friendly introduction to computers for students who do not have a Computer Science background. Computers have become an essential part of education, communication, business, research, and everyday life. Therefore, understanding basic computer concepts is useful for students from every academic discipline.

The first objective of this project is to introduce the fundamentals of computers. Students will learn what a computer is, how it works, and why computers are important in modern society. The project explains basic concepts using simple language so that beginners can understand them without requiring prior technical knowledge.

The second part of the agenda focuses on the major components of a computer. Students will become familiar with devices such as the Central Processing Unit (CPU), monitor, keyboard, and mouse. The purpose and basic function of each component will be explained. This will help students understand how different hardware components work together to perform tasks.

Another important objective is to introduce students to the basic working process of a computer. The project explains how a computer receives input, processes information, stores data, and produces output. Understanding this input-process-output cycle provides students with a foundation for learning more advanced computer concepts in the future.

The project also covers the generations of computers. Students will learn about the development of computers from the first generation, which used vacuum tubes, to later generations that introduced transistors, integrated circuits, microprocessors, and modern intelligent technologies. This historical overview helps students understand how computers have evolved in terms of size, speed, reliability, and functionality.

The website is also designed to encourage self-learning. Instead of presenting information in a highly technical manner, the project organizes the content into simple sections that students can explore at their own pace. The navigation menu provides access to different sections such as Home, Fundamentals, Agenda, and Contact Us.

Another agenda of the project is to demonstrate the use of basic web development technologies. The website is developed using HTML for creating the structure and CSS for designing and presenting the content. Through this project, students can also gain an introductory understanding of how web pages are created and styled.

The Contact Us section provides an opportunity for users to submit their name, email address, and message. Basic HTML form validation is included to demonstrate how websites can collect information in a structured manner.

Overall, this project aims to make computer education accessible to non-CS students by presenting fundamental concepts in a clear and organized way. It serves as a starting point for students who want to become more comfortable with computers and develop the basic digital knowledge required for academic, professional, and everyday activities.
